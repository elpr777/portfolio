Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what importers and distributors ask most.

Do your products meet GCC and EU import requirements?

Yes. Our plants hold ISO 22000 and FSSC 22000, and we supply the certificate of origin and lab reports with every shipment in line with the importing country’s regulators.

What is the minimum order?

Usually one mixed pallet for ready products and a 20 ft container for private label. The final figure depends on the product and destination.

Do you offer private label manufacturing?

Yes, for olive oil, honey, tahini, canned goods and dates. We help with design, specifications and registration in the target market.

What is the shelf life of your products?

Between 12 and 36 months depending on the product, shown on each product page and on the pack.

How can I trace a product’s origin?

Every pack carries a batch code. Send it to our quality team and we reply within one working day with the farm, harvest and packing dates, and test results.

How long do preparation and shipping take?

Orders are usually ready in 10 to 14 working days, and sea freight takes 5 to 15 days depending on the port of arrival.

Ready to start a partnership?

Send us your requirement and our sales team will reply with a quote and specifications within one working day.
