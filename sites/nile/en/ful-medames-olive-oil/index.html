Ful medames with olive oil and lemon

A classic Egyptian breakfast in ten minutes from a can of ful and a few simple ingredients.

Ful medames needs very little to become a complete meal. What matters is good oil and a squeeze of fresh lemon.

Ingredients

  • 1 can Nile Harvest Ful Medames (400 g)
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • 1 crushed garlic clove, salt and cumin
  • Tomato and parsley to garnish

Method

  1. Warm the beans gently with the garlic and cumin for five minutes.
  2. Pour into a wide dish and mash lightly with a fork.
  3. Add lemon juice, olive oil, tomato and parsley, and serve hot with baladi bread.
